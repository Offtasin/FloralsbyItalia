# Google Business Profile setup

Takes about 15 minutes, plus waiting for Google to verify the business.
Use the Google account that should own the listing.

## 1. Create the profile

1. Go to **business.google.com** and tap **Manage now**.
2. Business name: **Florals by Italia**
3. Category: **Florist**
4. "Do you want to add a location customers can visit?" Pick **No**.
   (You work from home, so the address stays hidden.)
5. Service areas: add **East Los Angeles**, **Los Angeles**, **Boyle Heights**,
   **Montebello**, **Monterey Park**, **Commerce**, plus any others you deliver to.
6. Phone: **(323) 842-0960**
7. Website: **https://floralsbyitalia.vercel.app** (switch to your own domain if you get one)
8. Verify. Google usually asks for a short video showing the business: film
   your workspace, flowers and supplies, and a bouquet you're making.

## 2. Fill in the details (copy and paste)

**Description** (up to 750 characters):

> Florals by Italia makes custom, hand-wrapped bouquets in East Los Angeles. Tell us the occasion, colors and flowers they love, and we'll design a bouquet around them and send you an exact price before you pay. From a sweet dozen to a full ramo buchón with 100+ roses, every bouquet is made to order for birthdays, anniversaries, Valentine's Day, Mother's Day, graduations and quinceañeras. Add a satin ribbon with gold glitter lettering in English or Spanish. Pickup in East LA and delivery around Los Angeles. Hablamos español. Text 323-842-0960 to order.

**Opening date:** the month you started taking orders.

**Hours:** set the hours you answer texts. If you only work by appointment,
choose "Open with no main hours."

**More categories** (optional): *Flower designer*, *Gift shop*.

**Services** (add each with a starting price):

| Service | Price |
|---|---|
| Small bouquet, 10–12 stems | from $50 |
| Medium bouquet, about 25 stems | from $85 |
| Large bouquet, about 50 stems | from $140 |
| Grand bouquet / ramo buchón, 100+ stems | from $250 |
| Ribbon with gold lettering | $8 |
| Local delivery | from $15 |

**Attributes:** turn on *Identifies as women-owned* and *Latino-owned* if they apply,
plus *Online appointments* and *Onsite services* as fits.

**Booking / order link:** https://floralsbyitalia.vercel.app/order.html

## 3. Photos (the biggest boost)

- **Logo:** `assets/img/logo-512.png`
- **Cover:** a wide photo of your best bouquet
- **At least 10 more:** the 12 bouquets on the site plus the customer photos.
  Add 2–3 new ones every week or two. Fresh photos help you show up in searches.

## 4. Reviews

- In the profile, tap **Ask for reviews** to get a link.
- Text that link to happy customers right after pickup, while they're excited:
  > "Thank you for ordering! If you loved your bouquet, a quick Google review helps
  > my small business a lot 💐 [link]"
- Reply to every review, even with a short thank-you.

## 5. After it's live

- Add the Google review link to your Instagram bio.
- Post holiday updates (Valentine's Day, Mother's Day) in the profile's **Updates**
  section about 3 weeks before the date.
- Once you have a few reviews, they can go on the website too.
