How ordering works

No checkout and no guessing. You tell us what you want and we send you an exact price.

  1. Send your request

    Fill out the order form or text us. Add a photo if you have a look in mind.

  2. Get your quote

    We'll reply with a design idea and an exact price, usually within a day.

  3. Pay a 20% deposit

    The deposit holds your date and lets us order your flowers. The rest is due at pickup or delivery.

  4. Pick up or get it delivered

    Fresh the day you need it, wrapped and ready to hand over.

Questions

Don't see yours? Text us at 323-842-0960.

How far ahead should I order?

Three days ahead works for most bouquets. For 100 stems and up, and for Valentine's Day or Mother's Day, order at least a week ahead.

How does the deposit work?

Once you approve your quote, a 20% deposit holds your date and lets us buy your flowers. The rest is due at pickup or delivery.

Why do prices go up on holidays?

Flower prices jump the week of Valentine's Day, Mother's Day and other big holidays, so holiday bouquets cost more than the starting prices above.

Can you match a photo I found?

Yes. Send the photo with your request and we'll tell you what it takes to make it, or suggest swaps that fit your budget.

Where do you deliver?

We're based in East Los Angeles and deliver around LA. The delivery fee depends on distance and is included in your quote.

How do I keep it fresh?

Trim the stems at an angle, put them in cool water and change the water every couple of days. Keep the bouquet out of direct sun.
